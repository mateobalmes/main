🎵 TikTok-Trending in Tokyo
teamLab Planets — consistently one of the most-shared experiences on travel TikTok. Barefoot immersive art, koi fish turn into flowers, mirror floors. Book in advance. Evening slots are quieter on weekends.
Shibuya NPC trend — standing still in Shibuya Scramble like an NPC while others cross. Viral TikTok trend. Free to participate — just stand in the middle and film reactions.
Shibuya Sky rooftop — open-air observation deck on top of Shibuya Scramble Square. Sunset slot sells out a month ahead. One of the most-shared Tokyo views on TikTok. ~¥2,200.
Cake in a can vending machine — in Shibuya 109 ground floor, near escalators. Literally sponge cake in a can from a vending machine. Very TikTok.
Gachapon at Sunshine City, Ikebukuro — world's largest gachapon (capsule toy) store. Floors of machines. Very popular on TikTok. Buy one of everything.
Shimokitazawa thrift shopping — Tokyo's indie thrift and vintage hub, trending as an alternative to Harajuku. Good for finds alongside the vintage camera hunt.
Shodai White Curry Udon — Instagrammable and TikTok-viral noodle dish. Multiple Tokyo locations. Visually striking, actually delicious.
Go-karting through Shibuya Scramble — extremely viral. Driving a kart through the actual Shibuya Crossing in costume. Need IDP booklet.
Yukata rental at Asakusa — summer yukata (lighter than kimono) + Senso-ji visit. Huge on TikTok as a solo cultural experience. Kimono Rental Ann near the temple.
Hotel Chinzanso Tokyo garden — free to enter. Japanese garden from 1878 with a 3-story pagoda and 500-year-old sacred tree. TikTok calls it a "hidden gem tourists haven't found."
