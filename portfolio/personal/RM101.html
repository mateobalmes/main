Perishable

Like a concert ticket or fresh bread: once the door closes, an unsold seat can't be stored and sold later.

The vocabulary

Every decision is a balancing act

Risk ←

Spill

Higher-paying customers turned away because the seats were already sold cheaper.

Risk ←

Dilution

Selling a seat for less than the customer was willing to pay.

Risk →

Stifle

Holding seats for late, high-fare customers who never show up.

Risk →

Spoilage

Seats that fly empty, including no-shows we didn't plan for.

Denied boarding

We overbooked for no-shows, and more people showed up than seats.

Market share

Sometimes we price to protect our place in a market.

03 · People

The people behind RM

O&D
The forecaster

Demand Analyst

Predicts how many people will want each journey, and what they'll be willing to pay.

  • Builds a view of every market
  • Checks what the RM system forecasts and recommends
  • Adjusts when the forecast doesn't match reality or strategy
Where you'll feel itFare availability opening or closing as demand changes.
O&D
The architect

Pricing Analyst

Designs the fares, the price points, and the rules that come with them.

  • Builds the fare structure for each market
  • Uses fare rules ("fences") like advance purchase or change fees to separate customer types
  • Benchmarks against competitors and files fares to every sales channel
Where you'll feel itThe fares and fare rules you see in the GDS and NDC.
LEG
The flight manager

Flight Revenue Analyst

Makes sure each individual flight fills at the right pace.

  • Manages how fast flights fill (booking load factor)
  • Sets overbooking levels
  • Handles groups, negotiated fares, Aeroplan redemptions and interline
Where you'll feel itGroup requests, oversold flights and Aeroplan availability.

Take it back to your desk

Five things to remember

1

RM sells the right seat to the right customer at the right price at the right time.

2

Prices move because seats are perishable, capacity is fixed, and every customer values a seat differently.

3

RM manages the whole journey (O&D), not one flight, so availability can differ by itinerary.

4

Three core roles: Demand Analysts, Pricing Analysts and Flight Revenue Analysts.

5

RM works hand in hand with the commercial teams, including you. That's our competitive advantage.
