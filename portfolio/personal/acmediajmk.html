Gate 01

What this role actually is

Different beast from the Partnerships Advisor role — this one is a data and measurement job, not a relationship job.

AC Media is Air Canada's advertising business — it sells marketing inventory (including the in-flight entertainment channel) to third-party brands, the same category as a retail media network like Walmart Connect or Air Canada's peers running loyalty-based ad businesses. This role sits on the analytics side of that business, reporting into a structure that works alongside a Data Scientist, AC Media. Three things this job is really testing for:

  • Measurement rigor: building the reporting, test/control structure, and KPI frameworks that prove a campaign worked — this is the entire point of the role.
  • Vendor-facing translation: turning raw data into something a sales team and an external advertiser can act on and trust.
  • Data infrastructure fluency: tagging, data availability, and working with a Data Scientist to make sure the underlying data is even trustworthy before it's reported.

It's Senior Level, wants 5+ years or a degree in a quantitative field, and is explicitly heavier on digital marketing / ad-tech vocabulary than the Partnerships Advisor posting was. This is the more technical of the two roles — lean into that.

Gate 02

Lingo to have ready

This posting is dense with digital marketing terms that don't show up in Revenue Management. Know these cold.

Retail Media Network (RMN)

An advertising business built on top of a company's own audience/data — AC Media is Air Canada's version, same category as Amazon Ads or Walmart Connect.

Test/control strategy

Holding out a control group so you can prove a campaign caused the lift, not just correlated with it — the posting names this directly.

Tagging

Placing tracking pixels/tags so campaign data is actually captured — "ensure necessary tagging and data availability" is a listed responsibility.

Audience profiling

Describing who a segment of customers actually is, used to sell advertisers on why AC's audience is worth targeting.

Post-campaign measurement

Proving what a campaign delivered after it ran — the core deliverable of this entire role.

Ad servers

The systems that deliver and track ads (e.g. Google Ad Manager) — listed as a nice-to-have environment.

Funnel optimization

Improving conversion at each stage from awareness to purchase — a CRM/digital marketing fundamental named in the posting.

CPC / CAC

Cost per click / customer acquisition cost — the KPI language advertisers speak in, explicitly listed as required knowledge.

Data Scientist, AC Media

The role you'd work alongside — they likely own modeling depth, you own translation, reporting and stakeholder delivery.

IFE channel

In-flight entertainment — one of AC Media's unique ad inventory surfaces, worth name-checking as understanding the product.

A good question to ask back

This role is new-ish territory (retail media networks at airlines are a growing category) — a question about maturity and roadmap plays well.

  • Best single option: "How mature is the tagging and data infrastructure today for AC Media's channels, including IFE — is this role more about building that foundation or optimizing an existing one?"
  • Strong alternative: "How is success on this team currently measured — is it primarily proving value to advertisers, or also building AC Media's own internal case for investment?"
  • Backup: "What does the split in ownership look like day to day between this role and the Data Scientist, AC Media?"
