Gate 01

What this role actually is

The shape of the job, not the posting text.

This sits inside Travel and Loyalty Platforms, supporting Aeroplan's travel partnerships — day-one focus is the car rental vertical, with room to grow. Three layers working at once:

  • Commercial: business case analysis, forward and historic modeling, partnership profitability, program codes and promo T&Cs.
  • Analytical: data models across customer, operational and revenue data to find optimization opportunities, plus market and competitor benchmarking in travel and loyalty.
  • Relational: connective tissue between Digital Product/IT, Partner Marketing and CRM, Aeroplan Finance, customer care, and the partner itself. Closer to an account manager who can also build the model than a pure analyst.

Three years of relevant experience is the real bar, not airline/loyalty tenure specifically — the posting wants someone curious about aviation and loyalty, not already expert in it. As an internal mover already inside the airline's commercial engine, that bar is comfortably cleared.

Gate 02

Lingo to have ready

You already speak most of this fluently from Revenue Management — this is the partnerships-side vocabulary layered on top.

Accrual vs. Redemption

Earning Aeroplan points vs. spending them. Partnerships sit on one or both sides.

Breakage

Points issued but never redeemed — loyalty's version of the revenue recognition question.

Activation

A member actually using a partner benefit after seeing it marketed, not just awareness.

Value proposition

What Aeroplan is worth to a member vs. competitors — the framing this role is told to amplify.

Partner P&L

The commercial split between Air Canada/Aeroplan and the partner.

Program codes

Operational tagging used to track a specific partnership offer or campaign — directly adjacent to the OTA promo work you've already done.

Co-brand card

TD and CIBC's Aeroplan cards — the largest loyalty partnership by revenue.

Affinity analysis

Correlating usage patterns to find partnership fit — you've built this exact analysis at Globe.

BLF / Yield

Booking load factor and yield — already your daily vocabulary from RM.

Digital Product team

The internal team building the tech surface partnerships live on — your business-side counterpart.

Gate 07

A good question to ask back

One sharp question beats three safe ones — this one forces a real answer, not a script.

  • Best single option: "Car rental is the primary focus today — what does the roadmap look like for expanding into other partnership categories, and how does this role's scope evolve as that happens?"
  • Strong alternative, given your affinity-analysis background: "How does the team currently decide which new partnership categories to pursue — is it opportunity-driven from data, or more relationship-driven from inbound partner interest?"
  • Backup: "Where does the friction usually show up between Partner Marketing, Digital Product and this role, and how does the team resolve it?"
