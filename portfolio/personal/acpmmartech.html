What to say

Opening, 40 seconds

I'm a product manager at TD's AI centre of excellence, where I run enterprise AI intake and partnership strategy across research, engineering, product and risk. Before that I was Senior Analyst, Marketing Technology at Restaurant Brands, working in Adobe Experience Platform and Customer Journey Analytics on audience creation and activation, alongside hands-on build work in Campaign Classic and Journey Optimizer. What draws me to this role is that it puts the roadmap and the platform in the same pair of hands, which is where I do my best work.

Why you are leaving TD

Layer 6 has been a good run on the strategy side of AI delivery, and I've enjoyed the enterprise view. What I want back is direct ownership of a platform, end to end. This role is one of the few I've seen that asks for the product direction and the hands-on build together rather than splitting them.

What to ask

Pick three, then close on the last one.

  • The posting draws a firm line between Marketing Technology owning the platforms and Marketing Operations running campaigns. How settled is that split today? The unusual clarity suggests it was recently contested. Their answer tells you what you would walk into.
  • What is driving the timing on the Campaign v7 to v8 move, and where is it in the plan? Tests whether this is a migration hire with a deadline attached.
  • Who does this role report to, and how big is the Marketing Technology team?
  • What does the first six months look like if it goes well?
  • What are the next steps and the timeline? Always close on this.
